About the process: what changed from v1

Before: three separate store spreadsheets, no totals, no ROI, approvals based on experience.

Now: one combined record for every investment, with ROI = (Revenue − Maintenance − Cost) ÷ Cost calculated automatically, filters by store and asset type, and status flags.
