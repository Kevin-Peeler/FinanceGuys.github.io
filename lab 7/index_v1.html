Capital Investment Log

How WOC tracks equipment and facility investments today: one spreadsheet tab per store, updated by hand.

About the Process (As-Is)

  1. A store manager emails the Finance Director asking to buy an asset (e-bikes, skis, a van, a remodel).
  2. The request is approved based on the manager's experience. There is no standard ROI analysis.
  3. The purchase is typed into that store's spreadsheet tab.
  4. Revenue and maintenance are added at month-end from separate reports.
  5. Nobody compares performance across stores before the next purchase.

Known Problems

  • No ROI calculation or totals. Managers have to work them out by hand.
  • Data is split across three store tabs.
  • Poor investments aren't flagged, so the same mistakes repeat.
  • Executives can't see which assets work at which location.
